SourceZero GWM

This is the document reading surface of CHORA. In the real system this area is populated directly from the normalized archive, while semantic metadata, concepts and relations remain available around the document.

The original document is preserved as the primary source. Everything derived by CHORA — embeddings, concepts, entities and inferred relations — belongs to a separate semantic layer and can be regenerated without changing the source.

The document is the atom. The graph is the relation. The semantic space is the continuum.

This separation keeps the archive deterministic and lets intelligence operate on top of CHORA instead of becoming a dependency of the archive itself.
